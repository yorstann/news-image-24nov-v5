# 📰 THE WORLD TODAY — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A clean classic newspaper front page summing up 24 November 2026 in one image. Light cream paper, a hand-drawn serif typeface, and a strict hierarchy: one dominant lead — COP31 talks collapse in Antalya — a second hero on the warship sunk off Benghazi, and a compact "Around the World" section with a pinned world map and the day in numbers.

**Stories:** COP31 collapse (Antalya) · warship sunk off Benghazi · 3 missiles breach Polish airspace · Nvidia rebounds 31.5% · Indonesia seals a trade deal · U.S.–Iran tensions rise.

**Build:** `node main.ts` renders the page with a self-contained rasterizer and serif font (no external images, no network) and writes a deterministic 1024×1024 PNG to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (two runs are byte-identical).
